Refresh from your store

The page cannot read the store itself: the offers only exist in your signed-in browser. Three clicks do it without a Claude session:

  1. Once: drag this link to your bookmarks bar: MSF: grab my offers
  2. Open your store (signed in), then click the bookmark there. It renders every card, which takes a minute or two, and copies the offers.
  3. Come back here, paste into the box below and click Import pasted offers. The ranking updates at once; to keep it for next time, also paste it into data/offers.js or ask a Claude session to run /msf-store-pull.

Offers otherwise load from data/offers.js when the page opens. Click an offer to see its breakdown.

Scoring chart

Value per US$ is the one number: the offer's contents at msfwar's average web-store prices, divided by its price in US dollars, with your plan applied when Personalize is on. 1× means priced like the average offer for the same things.

Best buys by resource

For each resource in your store, the offer that sells it cheapest. "If this is all you want" counts the offer's whole price against that one resource, which is what you actually pay for it. "Inside a bundle" charges each item its share of the price in proportion to its msfwar value, so a bundle's gold can look very cheap when the rest of the bundle carries the value; read it as "the bundle, not the gold, is the deal".
