Refresh from your store

The page cannot read the store itself: the offers only exist in your signed-in browser. A bookmark does the reading, and no Claude session is needed:

  1. Once, and again whenever this tool is updated (an old copy reads less): drag this link to your bookmarks bar, replacing the old one: MSF: grab my offers
  2. Open your store (signed in) and click the bookmark there. It reads the page's own offer data at once, every item of every offer, and copies it.
  3. Then either double-click update.cmd in the tool folder and reload this page: it saves the read from the clipboard and rebuilds the values and the price history. Or paste into the box below and click Import pasted offers: the offers are ranked at once, but items new to the store have no price until update.cmd has run.

How to read the values

Usual prices come from your own earlier store reads and from nothing else. Each item's figure is what the offer is worth for that item alone, its value at the usual price divided by the offer's price, and the items add up to the full offer, which gets the grade. The switch at the top shows the same against the offer's Ultra Core price.

Shop by resource

Pick what you want to buy. Each offer that holds it is listed best first, with what the offer is worth for that resource alone and for everything in it. All lists every offer with a price.

Best buys by resource

For each resource in your store today: its usual price in your earlier reads, the most an earlier read gave for the money, and the offers that sell it cheapest today. "If this is all you want" counts the offer's whole price against that one resource, which is what you actually pay for it; the earlier reads are measured the same way, over the offers that were read completely. "Inside a bundle" charges each item its share of the price in proportion to its usual price, so an item in a bundle is as cheap as the bundle is good. The stat crystals of a tier are one resource, whatever their class and stat. A chain step that needs earlier steps first is left out: it cannot be bought on its own.
